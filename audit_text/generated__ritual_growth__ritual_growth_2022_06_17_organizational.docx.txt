Minutes of the Organizational Meeting of the Board of Directors
Ritual Growth, Inc.
(Board of Directors – DE corporation)

Meeting Details
Date of Meeting: 2022-06-17
Time of Meeting: 10:00 AM
Location of Meeting: Wayne, Pennsylvania, via digital communication
Address note: The principal address above is the Corporation’s designated notice and filing address on the corporate records. The Corporation is governed by the Delaware General Corporation Law; statutory references in these minutes follow that domicile. Place (and, where applicable, remote participation) reflects where the meeting was conducted for the stated date and may differ from the principal address.

I. Call to Order:
The Organizational Meeting of the Board of Directors of Ritual Growth, Inc. (the “Corporation”) was called to order at 10:00 AM on 2022-06-17 by Derek E. Pappas, acting as Chair of the Board, with Marija Cejovic also present as a director.

II. Roll Call and Quorum:
Directors Present:
Derek E. Pappas (Director)
Marija Cejovic (Director)
Directors Absent: None

With Derek E. Pappas and Marija Cejovic present—constituting the entire membership of the Board of Directors—a quorum was established and the meeting was duly constituted to transact business in accordance with the Delaware General Corporation Law. The Corporation’s governance practice is that all directors attend each board meeting. Notice for the meeting had been provided or waived as required, which the directors confirmed for the record.
The directors joined the meeting by approved digital means, with audio contemporaneous among participants, and such attendance was recorded as present in person at the meeting for quorum purposes.

III. Resolutions:
Upon consideration, the Board adopted the following resolutions:

Domestication / Continuation (Wyoming to Delaware)
RESOLVED, that the Board ratifies and approves the Corporation’s domestication / continuation from Wyoming to Delaware, effective June 10, 2022, and confirms that all acts taken to effect such domestication / continuation and to maintain the Corporation’s corporate existence and good standing are hereby ratified, confirmed, and approved in all respects. Filed domestication / continuation instruments are to be designated Exhibit A for attachment to these minutes upon filing with the minute book.

Ratification of Formation Actions
RESOLVED, that all actions taken to organize and form the Corporation, including filing the certificate/articles of incorporation and taking related organizational steps, are hereby ratified, confirmed, and approved in all respects.

Adoption of Bylaws
RESOLVED, that the bylaws described as By-Laws of Ritual Growth, Inc., to be designated Exhibit B for attachment to these minutes upon filing are hereby adopted as the bylaws of the Corporation, and the Secretary is authorized and directed to place a copy in the Corporation’s minute book.

Election of Officers
RESOLVED, that the following individuals are hereby elected and appointed to serve as officers of the Corporation, to serve at the pleasure of the Board and in accordance with the bylaws: Derek E. Pappas as President, Secretary, Treasurer, and Chief Executive Officer; Marija Cejovic as Chief Operating Officer.


In taking the actions reflected in these minutes, the directors relied in good faith on information, opinions, reports, and statements—including financial and operational materials prepared for this meeting and presentations from officers of the Corporation—as to matters the directors reasonably believed were within such persons’ professional or expert competence, as contemplated by DGCL § 141(e).


IV. Adjournment:
There being no further business to come before the Board, the meeting was adjourned.
