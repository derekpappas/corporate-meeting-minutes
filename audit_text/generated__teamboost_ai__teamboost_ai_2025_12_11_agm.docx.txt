II. Call to Order
The Annual Meeting of the Board of Directors of TeamBoost.ai, Inc. (the “Corporation”) was called to order at 1:00 PM on 2025-12-11 by Derek E. Pappas, acting as Chair of the Board, with Marija Cejovic also present as a director—all directors constituting the full membership of the Board were in attendance.

III. Roll Call and Quorum
Directors Present:

None

With Derek E. Pappas and Marija Cejovic present—constituting the entire membership of the Board of Directors—a quorum was established and the meeting was duly constituted to transact business in accordance with the Delaware General Corporation Law. The Corporation’s governance practice is that all directors attend each board meeting. The directors acknowledged on the record that notice requirements were satisfied by proper notice or waiver.
The directors participated via communications equipment by means of which all persons participating in the meeting could hear each other, and such participation constituted presence in person at the meeting.

IV. Approval of Prior Minutes
The minutes of the prior Annual Meeting of the Board of Directors held on 2024-12-12 were reviewed and approved by the Board.
V. Reports of Officers

President’s Report:
The directors reported on the Corporation’s operational and engineering activities for the calendar year, including centralized management of globally distributed development and the use of operational office location(s) during the calendar year, with operations conducted from Denver, North Carolina, USA; Lantana, Florida, USA; Wayne, Pennsylvania, USA and development from Brazil; Nigeria; Portugal, while confirming that management, oversight, and decision-making remained centralized and continuously recorded through the Corporation’s official records. The directors summarized continued product development during the year, including API servers and services, web applications, and mobile applications.  The Corporation continued to operate hardware and cloud infrastructure using hosting providers including DigitalOcean and Hetzner. The President’s report included the following accomplishments summary for the calendar year 2025: Release and stabilization phase: public rollout, improved reliability through testing, and continued enhancement of core features and system performance. A written addendum designated as Exhibit B for attachment to these minutes upon filing sets forth detailed accomplishments for the period covered by the President’s report, together with supplemental technical materials furnished for the meeting (including technical specifications, roadmaps, KPIs, and architecture diagrams) where applicable. All mobile clients, server-side services, and related intellectual property developed during the year were reaffirmed as the exclusive property of the Corporation.

Treasurer’s Report:
The Treasurer reported that the Corporation remains solvent and that certain outstanding obligations, including SAFEs, convertible notes, and similar instruments that remain contingent on a future liquidity event, the timing of which has not yet been determined. The directors acknowledged the status of such obligations and confirmed continued oversight of these matters. Franchise taxes and registered agent fees are paid and current. The Corporation has 10,000,000 shares of common stock issued and outstanding at a par value of $.0001 per share.

In taking the actions reflected in these minutes, the directors relied in good faith on information, opinions, reports, and statements—including financial and operational materials prepared for this meeting and presentations from officers of the Corporation—as to matters the directors reasonably believed were within such persons’ professional or expert competence, as contemplated by DGCL § 141(e).


VI. Discussion Items
The directors discussed mobile product velocity and infrastructure scaling for 2026, including app-store release cadence, on-device performance budgets, and push-notification reliability targets.

VII. Resolutions
Upon consideration, the Board adopted the following resolutions:

Approval of Financial Reports
RESOLVED, that the financial statements for calendar year 2025 are hereby approved.

Approval of 2026 Budget
RESOLVED, that the operating, engineering, and marketing budget for calendar year 2026 is hereby approved.

Banking Authorization
RESOLVED, that Derek E. Pappas is authorized to open, maintain, and manage one or more corporate bank accounts in the name of the Corporation at First Citizens Bank & Trust Company, and any successor institution, and to act as the sole authorized signatory with full authority to execute all related documents.



Stockholder Written Consent (2025)
The Board noted that the Written Consent of Sole Stockholder dated December 11, 2025, adopting stockholder resolutions under DGCL §228 for the year 2025, will be filed with the minutes of the stockholders of the Corporation upon execution, to be designated Exhibit A upon filing.


VIII. Adjournment
There being no further business to come before the Board, the meeting was adjourned.
