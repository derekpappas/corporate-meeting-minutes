Minutes of the Organizational Meeting of the Board of Directors
Hippo, Inc.
(Board of Directors – DE corporation)

Meeting Details
Date of Meeting: 2022-04-14
Time of Meeting: 10:00 AM
Location of Meeting: Sunny Isles, Florida, via digital communication
Address note: The principal address above is the Corporation’s designated notice and filing address on the corporate records. The Corporation is governed by the Delaware General Corporation Law; statutory references in these minutes follow that domicile. Place (and, where applicable, remote participation) reflects where the meeting was conducted for the stated date and may differ from the principal address.

I. Call to Order:
The Organizational Meeting of the Board of Directors of Hippo, Inc. (the “Corporation”) was called to order at 10:00 AM on 2022-04-14 by Derek E. Pappas, acting as Chair of the Board, with Marija Cejovic also present as a director.

II. Roll Call and Quorum:
Directors Present:
Derek E. Pappas (Director)
Marija Cejovic (Director)
Directors Absent: None

The directors acknowledged on the record that notice requirements were satisfied by proper notice or waiver.
All directors (Derek E. Pappas, Marija Cejovic) were present, the Board was fully constituted, and quorum requirements under the Delaware General Corporation Law and the Corporation’s bylaws were satisfied. Each director is expected to attend every board meeting, and that expectation was met for this meeting.
Remote participation was used; the means employed allowed contemporaneous hearing among participants and were treated as satisfying any applicable in-person presence requirement for each director.

III. Resolutions:
Upon consideration, the Board adopted the following resolutions:

Ratification of Formation Actions
RESOLVED, that all actions taken to organize and form the Corporation, including filing the certificate/articles of incorporation and taking related organizational steps, are hereby ratified, confirmed, and approved in all respects.

Adoption of Bylaws
RESOLVED, that the bylaws described as Amended and Restated Bylaws of Hippo, Inc., to be designated Exhibit A for attachment to these minutes upon filing are hereby adopted as the bylaws of the Corporation, and the Secretary is authorized and directed to place a copy in the Corporation’s minute book.

Election of Officers
RESOLVED, that the following individuals are hereby elected and appointed to serve as officers of the Corporation, to serve at the pleasure of the Board and in accordance with the bylaws: Derek E. Pappas as President, Secretary, Treasurer, and Chief Executive Officer; Marija Cejovic as Chief Operating Officer.


In taking the actions reflected in these minutes, the directors relied in good faith on information, opinions, reports, and statements—including financial and operational materials prepared for this meeting and presentations from officers of the Corporation—as to matters the directors reasonably believed were within such persons’ professional or expert competence, as contemplated by DGCL § 141(e).


IV. Adjournment:
There being no further business to come before the Board, the meeting was adjourned.
