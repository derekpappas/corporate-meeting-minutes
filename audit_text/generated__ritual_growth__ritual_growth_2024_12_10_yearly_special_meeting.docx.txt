
Minutes of the Special Meeting of the Board of Directors - 2024
Ritual Growth, Inc.
(Board of Directors – DE corporation)

Meeting Details
Date of Meeting: 2024-12-10
Time of Meeting: 12:00 PM
Location of Meeting: Denver, NC, via digital communication
Address note: The principal address above is the Corporation’s designated notice and filing address on the corporate records. The Corporation is governed by the Delaware General Corporation Law; statutory references in these minutes follow that domicile. Place (and, where applicable, remote participation) reflects where the meeting was conducted for the stated date and may differ from the principal address.
Purpose: Pre-annual review of product delivery, hosting footprint, and partner commitments

I. Call to Order:
The Special Meeting of the Board of Directors of Ritual Growth, Inc. (the “Corporation”) was called to order at 12:00 PM on 2024-12-10 by Derek E. Pappas, acting as Chair of the Board, with Marija Cejovic also present as a director.

II. Roll Call and Quorum:
Directors Present:
Derek E. Pappas (Director)
Marija Cejovic (Director)
Directors Absent: None

Attendance by Derek E. Pappas and Marija Cejovic satisfied quorum as the full Board; the meeting lawfully convened under the Delaware General Corporation Law. No director was absent. The directors confirmed that notice had been duly given or validly waived for this meeting.
The directors attended using remote communications by which each participant could hear the others, and the Board treated that participation as presence in person where permitted by applicable law and the Corporation’s bylaws.

III. Resolutions:
Upon consideration, the Board adopted the following resolution:

Ratification of Product Delivery and Hosting Operations
RESOLVED, that engineering, hosting, and go-to-market decisions affecting the Corporation’s web applications and API services during 2024 are hereby ratified, confirmed, and approved in all respects.


In taking the actions reflected in these minutes, the directors relied in good faith on information, opinions, reports, and statements—including financial and operational materials prepared for this meeting and presentations from officers of the Corporation—as to matters the directors reasonably believed were within such persons’ professional or expert competence, as contemplated by DGCL § 141(e).


IV. Adjournment:
There being no further business to come before the Board, the meeting was adjourned.
