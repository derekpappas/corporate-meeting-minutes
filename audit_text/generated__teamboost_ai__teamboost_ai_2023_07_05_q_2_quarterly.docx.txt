
Minutes of the Quarterly Governance Meeting - 2023 Q2
TeamBoost.ai, Inc.
(Board of Directors – DE corporation)

Meeting Details
Date of Meeting: 2023-07-05
Time of Meeting: 2:00 PM
Location of Meeting: Wayne, Pennsylvania, via digital communication
Address note: The principal address above is the Corporation’s designated notice and filing address on the corporate records. The Corporation is governed by the Delaware General Corporation Law; statutory references in these minutes follow that domicile. Place (and, where applicable, remote participation) reflects where the meeting was conducted for the stated date and may differ from the principal address.

I. Call to Order:
The Quarterly Governance Meeting of the Board of Directors of TeamBoost.ai, Inc. (the “Corporation”) was called to order at 2:00 PM on 2023-07-05 by Derek E. Pappas, acting as Chair of the Board, with Marija Cejovic also present as a director.

II. Roll Call and Quorum:
Directors Present:
Derek E. Pappas (Director)
Marija Cejovic (Director)
Directors Absent: None

All directors (Derek E. Pappas, Marija Cejovic) were present, the Board was fully constituted, and quorum requirements under the Delaware General Corporation Law and the Corporation’s bylaws were satisfied. Each director is expected to attend every board meeting, and that expectation was met for this meeting. The directors confirmed that notice of the meeting was duly given or waived.
The directors attended using remote communications by which each participant could hear the others, and the Board treated that participation as presence in person where permitted by applicable law and the Corporation’s bylaws.

III. Business Review:
The directors reviewed quarterly infrastructure stability and confirmed that all assets, including software and related intellectual property, created during the quarter in the development centers located in Brazil; Nigeria; Portugal are properly titled to and are the exclusive property of the Corporation.

In taking the actions reflected in these minutes, the directors relied in good faith on information, opinions, reports, and statements—including financial and operational materials prepared for this meeting and presentations from officers of the Corporation—as to matters the directors reasonably believed were within such persons’ professional or expert competence, as contemplated by DGCL § 141(e).


IV. Resolution:
Upon consideration, the Board adopted the following resolution:

RESOLVED, that all mobile, API, and supporting cloud infrastructure work completed during the quarter—and related intellectual property—is hereby ratified, confirmed, and approved as assets of the Corporation.



V. Adjournment:
There being no further business to come before the Board, the meeting was adjourned.

Signed:
Derek E. Pappas

Signed:
Marija Cejovic
