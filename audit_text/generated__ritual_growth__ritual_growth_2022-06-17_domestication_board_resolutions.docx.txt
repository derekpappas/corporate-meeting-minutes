Standalone board resolutions — Ritual Growth, Inc.
Related board meeting date (cycle anchor): 2022-06-17
Subject: Domestication / continuation (Wyoming to Delaware)

Prior jurisdiction (Wyoming) — ratification through domestication
RESOLVED, that all corporate acts taken by or on behalf of the Corporation while domiciled in Wyoming through the effectiveness of its domestication / continuation into Delaware are hereby ratified, confirmed, and approved in all respects, to the extent permitted by applicable law.

Domestication / continuation (Wyoming to Delaware) — approval
RESOLVED, that the Board hereby approves and confirms the Corporation’s domestication / continuation from Wyoming to Delaware, effective June 10, 2022, and ratifies and approves all acts taken to effect such domestication / continuation and to maintain the Corporation’s corporate existence and good standing in Delaware, in each case in all respects.

Domestication / continuation — instruments and minute book
RESOLVED, that Filed domestication / continuation instruments are to be designated Exhibit A for attachment upon filing. FURTHER RESOLVED, that the Secretary is authorized and directed to file, index, or cross-file such instruments with the Corporation’s minute book and related corporate records as counsel may advise.
