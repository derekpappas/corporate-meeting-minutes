Meeting Details

I. Call to Order:
The Special Meeting of the Board of Directors of SurveyTeams, Inc. (the “Corporation”) was called to order at 12:00 PM on 2026-12-08 by Derek E. Pappas, acting as Chair of the Board, with Mohamed Mohamed also present as a director.

II. Roll Call and Quorum:
Directors Present:
Derek E. Pappas (Director)
Mohamed Mohamed (Director)
Directors Absent: None

With Derek E. Pappas and Mohamed Mohamed present—constituting the entire membership of the Board of Directors—a quorum was established and the meeting was duly constituted to transact business in accordance with the Delaware General Corporation Law. The Corporation’s governance practice is that all directors attend each board meeting.
The directors confirmed that notice had been duly given or validly waived for this meeting.
The directors attended using remote communications by which each participant could hear the others, and the Board treated that participation as presence in person where permitted by applicable law and the Corporation’s bylaws.

III. Resolutions:
Upon consideration, the Board adopted the following resolutions:

Ratification of Survey Platform and Research Operations
RESOLVED, that engineering and operations decisions affecting the Corporation’s survey tooling, sampling workflows, and research compliance posture during 2026 are hereby ratified, confirmed, and approved in all respects.

Record Date for Annual Meeting of Stockholders (DGCL §213)
RESOLVED, that 2026-12-08 is hereby fixed as the record date for determining the stockholders entitled to notice of and to vote at the Annual Meeting of Stockholders of the Corporation to be held on 2026-12-18 commencing at 1:00 PM, in accordance with the Corporation’s bylaws and the Delaware General Corporation Law.


In taking the actions reflected in these minutes, the directors relied in good faith on information, opinions, reports, and statements—including financial and operational materials prepared for this meeting and presentations from officers of the Corporation—as to matters the directors reasonably believed were within such persons’ professional or expert competence, as contemplated by DGCL § 141(e).


IV. Adjournment:
There being no further business to come before the Board, the meeting was adjourned.
